# `guideline`

EBA Guidelines. Only one guideline in two languages, and the two files have different content.

## Documents

| ID | Title (API) | Pages |
|---|---|---:|
| `EBA-GL-2019-02_EN` | Final Report on EBA Guidelines on outsourcing arrangements (EBA/GL/2019/02) | 125 |
| `EBA-GL-2019-02_DE` | Leitlinien zu Auslagerungen (EBA/GL/2019/02) | 43 |

## Level 1: blocks

**Differs between DE and EN.**

| Section | EN begins with | DE begins with |
|---|---|---|
| `header` | `EBA/GL/2019/02` | `EBA/GL/2019/02` |
| `contents` | `Contents` | – |
| `executive_summary` | `Executive summary` | – |
| `background` | `Background` | – |
| `compliance_reporting` | `1. Compliance and reporting` | `1. Einhaltung und Meldepflichten` |
| `subject_scope_definitions` | `2. Subject matter, scope and definitions` | `2. Gegenstand, Anwendungsbereich …` |
| `implementation` | `3. Implementation` | `3. Umsetzung` |
| `guidelines` | `4. Guidelines on outsourcing` | `4. Leitlinien zu Auslagerungen` |
| `accompanying_documents` | `5. Accompanying documents` (cost-benefit analysis / impact assessment, feedback on the public consultation) | – |

- The EN file is the full Final Report; the DE file contains only `header` and the guidelines (`compliance_reporting` to `guidelines`).
- These four numbered sections are the common core. The consultation feedback in `accompanying_documents` is mostly tables (comment · EBA analysis · amendment).
- A running header (`FINAL REPORT ON THE GUIDELINES ON OUTSOURCING` / `ABSCHLUSSBERICHT ZU DEN LEITLINIEN ZU AUSLAGERUNGEN`) repeats on the pages.

## Level 2: grouping headings

**Same in DE and EN.** Block 4 is split into titles, the titles into numbered sections:

```
Title I – Proportionality …           / Titel I – Verhältnismäßigkeit …
  1  Proportionality                  / 1  Verhältnismäßigkeit
  2  Outsourcing by groups …          / 2  Auslagerung durch Gruppen …
Title II – Assessment of outsourcing arrangements
  3, 4
Title III – Governance framework
  5 … 11
Title IV – Outsourcing process
  12, 12.1–12.3, 13, 13.1–13.4, 14, 15
Title V – Guidelines on outsourcing addressed to competent authorities
```

- Section headings have the form `<number>  <text>` without a dot. Footnotes have the same form (`22 Die Institute sollten …`), so section headings and footnotes cannot be told apart by the number pattern alone.
- Footnote numbers differ between DE and EN because the EN file has more text before the guidelines.

## Level 3: numbered paragraphs

**Same in DE and EN.** Consecutive paragraph numbers `1.` to `119.` through the whole document: `1.`–`17.` before Title I, Title I starts at `18.`. E.g. `36. The management body is …` / `36. Das Leitungsorgan ist …`.
