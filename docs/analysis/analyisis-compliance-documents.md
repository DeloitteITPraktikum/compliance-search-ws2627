### Context

To support further processing, the 34 regulatory and supervisory documents must first be classified according to their structural characteristics. The documents differ significantly in scope, hierarchy, table density, annexes, formulas, and graphical elements. Treating all PDF files as one uniform document type would not adequately reflect these differences.

The analysis considers only the available document content and structures. Word counts that could not be reliably quantified are not included. Page counts marked as "approx." or "at least" are estimates based on the source material.

#### Document Classes and Structural Characteristics

##### 1\. European Primary and Secondary Legislation

**Associated documents:**

* `02013L0036-20260111\_EN.pdf` – CRD IV, consolidated English version
* `02013R0575-20260101\_DE.pdf` – CRR, consolidated German version
* `02013R0575-20260101\_EN.pdf` – CRR, consolidated English version
* `02023R1114-20240109\_DE.pdf` – MiCA, consolidated German version
* `02023R1114-20240109\_EN.pdf` – MiCA, consolidated English version
* `02024R1689-20240712\_DE.pdf` – AI Act, consolidated German version
* `32024R1689\_EN.pdf` – AI Act, English version
* `32022R2554\_DE.pdf` – DORA Regulation, German version
* `32022R2554\_EN.pdf` – DORA Regulation, English version
* `32016R0098\_EN.pdf` – Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/98

**Characteristics:**

* Scope ranges from approximately 10–20 pages to 891 pages.
* Strict legal hierarchy consisting of parts, titles, chapters, sections, articles, paragraphs, subparagraphs, and points.
* Extensive numbered recitals precede the normative main body.
* Annexes frequently contain classifications, thresholds, documentation requirements, or correlation tables.
* Table and formula density varies considerably. The CRR contains particularly large numbers of risk tables and mathematical formulas.
* German and English versions generally have identical article and paragraph structures.
* Standardised layout of the Official Journal of the European Union, including a CELEX identifier and EU emblem.

**Typical structure:**

1. Official Journal title page and CELEX header
2. Recitals
3. Main body of the legal act

   1. Parts
   2. Titles
   3. Chapters
   4. Sections
   5. Articles
   6. Paragraphs
   7. Subparagraphs and points
4. Annexes

##### 2\. Regulatory Technical Standards (RTS)

**Associated documents:**

* `32024R1772\_DE.pdf` / `32024R1772\_EN.pdf` – DORA RTS on ICT risk management
* `32024R1773\_DE.pdf` / `32024R1773\_EN.pdf` – DORA RTS on incident classification and reporting
* `32024R1774\_DE.pdf` / `32024R1774\_EN.pdf` – DORA RTS on oversight
* `32025R0301\_DE.pdf` / `32025R0301\_EN.pdf` – DORA RTS on subcontracting
* `32025R0420\_DE.pdf` / `32025R0420\_EN.pdf` – DORA RTS on Joint Examination Teams
* `32025R0532\_DE.pdf` / `32025R0532\_EN.pdf` – DORA RTS on subcontracting assessment

**Characteristics:**

* Compact to medium-length legal acts of approximately 8–30 pages.
* Provide technical and subject-specific detail for higher-level EU legislation.
* The analysed DORA RTS published in 2024 and 2025 show a high degree of editorial consistency.
* Primarily normative prose with catalogues of criteria and enumerations.
* Usually a low proportion of tables and no complex reporting forms or data matrices.
* Official Journal header and EU emblem on the first page.

**Typical structure:**

1. Official Journal title page
2. Recitals
3. Enacting formula
4. Articles with paragraphs and enumerations
5. Final provision
6. Signature line

##### 3\. Implementing Technical Standards (ITS)

**Associated documents:**

* `32024R2956\_EN.pdf` – DORA ITS on the register of information
* `32024R3172\_DE.pdf` / `32024R3172\_EN.pdf` – CRR ITS on disclosure templates
* `32025R0302\_DE.pdf` / `32025R0302\_EN.pdf` – DORA ITS on incident reporting templates

**Characteristics:**

* Scope of approximately 25–125 pages.
* Highest table density of all analysed document classes.
* Binding reporting and disclosure templates, data glossaries, and completion instructions.
* Logically linked tables using keys such as LEIs, function identifiers, or contractual arrangement reference numbers.
* Explicit technical field definitions, including alphanumeric data types, ISO 8601 timestamps, and closed lists.
* A short regulatory text followed by extensive annexes that contain most of the substantive content.

**Typical structure:**

1. Official Journal title page
2. Recitals and articles
3. Annex I containing instructions, completion guidance, and field catalogues
4. Additional annexes containing structured reporting templates

   1. Template identifier
   2. Predefined column headings
   3. Numbered rows and input fields

##### 4\. EBA Guidelines

**Associated documents:**

* `EBA-GL-2019-02\_DE.pdf` – EBA Guidelines on outsourcing, German version
* `EBA-GL-2019-02\_EN.pdf` – EBA Guidelines on Outsourcing, English version

**Characteristics:**

* At least 71 pages in length.
* Two-part structure consisting of a core supervisory section and accompanying documents.
* Includes impact assessments, cost-benefit analyses, and evaluations of public consultation responses.
* Tables are primarily used in the consultation section to compare comments, the EBA's analysis, and amendments.
* Recurring EBA logo in the header of many pages.
* Numbered sections and subpoints support precise references.

**Typical structure:**

1. EBA title page with logo and document reference
2. Executive summary
3. Background
4. Guidelines

   1. Compliance and reporting obligations
   2. Subject matter, scope, and definitions
   3. Date of application and transitional provisions
   4. Titles I–V with numbered subpoints
5. Accompanying documents

   1. Impact assessment or cost-benefit analysis
   2. Feedback from the public consultation

##### 5\. National Supervisory Circulars Issued by BaFin

**Associated documents:**

* `RS-05-2023-BA\_EN.pdf` – MaRisk 05/2023, English version
* `RS-06-2024-BA\_DE.pdf` – MaRisk 06/2024, German version
* `RS-10-2017-BA\_DE.pdf` – BAIT 10/2017, German version
* `RS-10-2017-BA\_EN.pdf` – BAIT 10/2017, English version
* `RS-10-2018-VA\_DE.pdf` – VAIT 10/2018, German version

**Characteristics:**

* Scope ranges from approximately 25 to more than 94 pages.
* Subject-specific modular structure. MaRisk uses the AT, BT, BTO, and BTR modules in particular.
* Consecutively numbered paragraphs enable precise references.
* Frequently includes a two-column table of contents listing modules and page numbers.
* Low table density in the main body; BaFin logo on the title page.
* Specifies German supervisory practice for banks and insurance undertakings.

**Typical structure:**

1. BaFin header with logo and circular reference
2. Addressees, date, and subject
3. Table of contents
4. Preliminary remarks or general section
5. Subject-specific modules with numbered paragraphs
6. Special section containing organisational and risk management requirements
7. Explanatory or supplementary notes

### Decision

For the initial analysis, the 34 documents are divided into five structural document classes:

1. European primary and secondary legislation
2. Regulatory Technical Standards (RTS)
3. Implementing Technical Standards (ITS)
4. EBA Guidelines
5. National supervisory circulars issued by BaFin

This classification consistently represents the different document structures. Language versions of the same legal act remain separate documents within the same class.

### Consequences

* The collection gains a consistent substantive and structural overview of all 34 documents.
* Common characteristics within a class, such as the EU legal hierarchy or the high table density of ITS documents, become immediately visible.
* Language versions can be compared based on their largely identical structures.
* The classification reduces complexity without completely losing document-specific characteristics.
* Individual borderline cases remain possible, particularly where a document contains both extensive legal text and highly structured annexes.
* Page counts remain estimates where the source material does not provide an unambiguous total.
* The classification represents the initial state of the analysis. Under the decision-record rules, changes to the classification must be documented in a new record that supersedes this one.
